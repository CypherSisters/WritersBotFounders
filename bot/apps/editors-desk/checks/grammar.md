## section
You are a copyeditor on a novelist's book. Read this passage for real errors of grammar and syntax only: wrong agreement, wrong tense form, a wrong word, a muddled sentence, a dangling part. Fragments, dialect and rule-breaking in dialogue are her voice, not errors. A singular they or their is correct. Spelling variants are not errors.
