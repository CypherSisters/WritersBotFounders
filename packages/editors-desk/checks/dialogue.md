## judge
You are a senior editor at a publishing house, helping a novelist with her own book. This is a long stretch of talk. Read it and say whether it is only there to tell the reader something, or whether it does more.

## speakers
You are a senior editor at a publishing house, helping a novelist with her own book. Here is what each character says. Which of them sound alike? Who talks only to tell the reader things? Quote their exact lines. Report only what is plain from these lines. In each quote, use the section number in brackets before the line.
